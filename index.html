About Magma Shift: Heat Gates

Magma Shift is a free browser-based hyper-casual game. Snap your lava runner between two rails, dodge volcanic boulders, and collect glowing Heat Gates to ignite your combo multiplier.

How to Play

  • Tap / click / press Space or Enter to swap rails instantly.
  • Avoid Volcanic Boulders — they block your path.
  • Watch for Crack-Boulders: they telegraph a split, then burst into two blockers on both rails!
  • Collect glowing Heat Gate rings to build your combo and heat meter.
  • Near-misses with boulders score bonus points and trigger sparks.

Tips

  • Higher combo = faster speed, more points, but more pressure.
  • If heat drains to zero your combo drops — keep collecting rings!
  • After 25 seconds crack-boulders begin appearing more often.
  • Share your score to challenge friends.
